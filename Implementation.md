Food is a mess to deal with , especially with multiple people accessing the same fridge. Usually a common approach is to split the shelves equally between the people living there, but this still creates a lot of challenges : The size of the shelves can be very small, some items can affect the food in other shelves, people mismanage their shelves and put food for a long time there, cross-contamination between meat products, and taking over other people's allocated space.

When living in shared rooms, that is usually the common approach. When living with a partner, the couple usually just share the whole fridge, but then that can cause issues as well when things are not totally coordinated.certain foods could be consumed while another person was holding on to them, people can have different hygiene standards and that can also cause fights about how to organize, and some people may be not knowledgeable on the diseases that can be spread by mashandling food in the fridge.

A solution would be to usually follow a specified official system that could gurantee the best for health and safety and comfort and satisfaction of the residents.
Another solution is to have a fixated reminder system that tracks expiry dates and klingelt when an item has expired
